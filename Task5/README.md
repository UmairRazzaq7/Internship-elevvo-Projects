# Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Business Report
## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset, which contains detailed information on orders, customers, products, payments, and reviews.
The goal of the analysis is to create an executive business summary that highlights key insights into sales, customer behavior, and product performance.

The report focuses on:

* Sales and revenue trends over time

* Customer demographics, purchasing patterns, and retention

* Product category performance and demand insights

* Payment methods and delivery efficiency

## 📊 Key Features

Data cleaning and preprocessing of the Olist dataset

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on sales, customers, and products

Visualization of key business metrics (sales, orders, categories, customers)

Executive report with insights and recommendations

Identification of growth opportunities and risk factors

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI (interactive dashboards and executive summaries)

Excel (quick analysis and reporting)


## 🚀 Outcomes

Developed a comprehensive business report from the Olist dataset.

Identified top-selling product categories and key customer segments.

Analyzed sales performance, seasonal trends, and payment methods.

Delivered actionable insights to improve sales growth, customer retention, and product strategy.
